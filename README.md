# Nissan 240SX (S13 / S14) for BeamNG.drive

A from-scratch BeamNG.drive vehicle mod of the Nissan 240SX: the S13 fastback (hatch), coupe and
convertible, and the S14 in early (zenki) and facelift (kouki) form. It comes with a large parts
catalogue and 38 preset builds, from bone-stock 1989 cars up to tube-chassis Honda K20A race cars at
about 700 hp.

Everything is generated by the Python tooling in `tools/` (headless Blender 4.5 for meshes, a
JBeam generator and an offline validator).

> Fan-made mod, not affiliated with or endorsed by Nissan or Honda. Real model and engine names are
> used for identification only; race liveries use fictional sponsors.

## Install

1. Download `dist/nissan_240sx_s13_s14.zip`.
2. Put the zip **as is** (do not extract it) in your BeamNG.drive user `mods` folder, for example
   `%LOCALAPPDATA%\BeamNG\BeamNG.drive\current\mods` on Windows.
3. Start the game, open the vehicle selector and pick **Nissan 240SX (S13)** or **Nissan 240SX (S14)**.

**Coupe and convertible bodies.** Both share every panel ahead of the B-pillar with the hatch, so all front
bumpers, hoods, fenders, pop-ups and doors fit every body. The coupe has its own roof, quarter windows, rear
window, trunk lid and wrap-around tail lamps. The convertible removes the roof structure and adds sill
stiffeners, floor X-braces and a belt-line hoop (about +90 kg, like the real car). Its power soft top is a
separate part: **Raised** (fabric over bows with a glass rear window and quarter windows, latched to the
windshield header) or **Folded** (stowed under the hard tonneau).

**Tube chassis.** This is a separate body part (`Tube Chassis (Race Spaceframe)`). It keeps every unibody
attachment point, so all suspension, engine and panel parts still fit. It adds a welded cage, a lighter frame,
an aluminium floor and tubs, and the visible tube-frame mesh is generated from the same nodes and beams.

**Aero (Tuning > Aerodynamics).**
- The swan-neck GT wing angle runs from 2° to 20°. It is set by beam precompression on the angle links, so the wing actually rotates.
- The splitter, dive planes and diffuser are triangle aero surfaces angled for downforce.
- The drag wicker bill is mostly drag with a small amount of downforce for high-speed stability.

**Drag electronics (Race Electronics slot).**
- **Transbrake:** Shift + Ctrl + Alt + Space by default.
- **Line lock:** locks the front brakes for burnouts; bind it in Controls.
- **Two-step:** comes with the race ECU (rev-limiter slider).
- **Shift lights:** 5-LED strip on the race tach.

**S14 body.** The S14 has its own body shape, traced from the S14 drawings: 2,525 mm wheelbase,
1,730 mm wide, longer and rounder than the S13. Physically it reuses the S13 coupe's structure, warped onto
the S14 dimensions; the unibody is about 60 kg heavier and stiffer in proportion. It has frameless doors
(roof and pillar seals stay on the body, the belt-line strip moves with the door), fixed headlamps and its own
dash: a rounded binnacle, twin centre vents, round outboard vents, three-dial climate control, the S14
gauge faces and the four-spoke airbag steering wheel.

- **Zenki / kouki parts** (mix and match, like the real kouki conversions):
  - **Hood:** zenki, kouki, vented FRP, carbon.
  - **Front bumper:** zenki or kouki, each stock, with a black aero lip, or with a carbon lip.
  - **Headlights:** zenki (clear) or kouki (projector, darker housing).
  - **Tail lamps:** zenki or kouki. The outer lamps are on the body; the inner lamps and reverse lights are on the trunk lid and
    open with it.
- **S14 only:** rear bumper with aero valance, three-post OEM wing with third brake light, ducktail lip, 15" and 16" S14 alloys.
- **Shared with the S13:** every engine, gearbox, differential, suspension, brake, wheel and tire, and the interior options
  (cloth, leather, stripped, race), cages, race aero, fuel cells and race electronics. The S14 uses its own copies with S14
  hard points, so S13 and S14 parts never mix by accident.

## Customisation (parts selector and Tuning menu)

- **Engines:** KA24E, KA24DE, SR20DET, K20A and the built K20A race engine.
  - Engine sub-slots: intake (stock, cold air, ITBs), manifold/header or turbo kit, ECU (race ECU with rev-limiter slider), forged internals, flywheel/clutch.
  - Turbo kits have a **wastegate pressure** slider.
- **Gearboxes:** 5MT (two ratio sets), 4AT, CD009 6MT, 6-speed sequential, 4-speed drag sequential. Every gear ratio is tunable.
- **Differentials:** open, viscous, 1.5-way and 2-way clutch LSD (preload and lock sliders), welded, spool. The final drive is tunable.
- **Suspension:** stock, sport, street, track, drift, drag and rally setups.
  - Ride height, spring and damper sliders on the coilovers.
  - Camber, toe and track-width sliders.
  - Sway bars: stock, sport, adjustable race, none.
- **Steering:** stock rack, quick rack, drift angle kit with extended lock.
- **Brakes:** stock, ABS, Z32 4-piston, 6-piston race kit, lightweight drag. Pads from street to full race.
  - Brake bias and brake strength sliders.
- **Wheels:** 11 designs from 14" to 18", including drag beadlocks and front runners.
- **Tires:** 18 sizes and compounds (all-season to drag slick), with tire pressure sliders.
- **Interior:** stock cloth, stripped (bucket seats), or race (carbon dash, race tach with shift lights, single race seat with 6-point harness, extinguisher, switch panel).
  - Steering wheels: stock, deep-dish, quick-release. Shifters: stock, short-throw, auto, sequential.
- **Cages:** 4-point roll bar, 6-point cage, full weld-in cage (unibody cars).
- **Race body parts:**
  - Vented FRP or carbon hood, carbon hatch with polycarbonate window, FRP doors with polycarbonate windows.
  - Wide-body front fenders (+50 mm), rear over-fenders, lightweight bumpers.
  - Pop-up delete with LED projectors.
- **Body kits:**
  - Front bumpers: stock, aero (lip spoiler), Type-X style drift (big intake, carbon lip), smooth drag, race (big intercooler opening).
  - Rear bumpers: stock, aero valance, race diffuser cut-out.
  - Also: side skirts, OEM hatch spoiler with a working third brake light, stock or aero side mirrors (working mirror cameras).
- **Liveries (Paint Design):** Factory Two-Tone, Twin Racing Stripes, IMSA GTO Tribute, Tractive Racing track livery, Drift Two-Tone.
  All are fully colourable: paint slots 1–3 recolour the livery colours.
- **Interior:** cloth (stock) or leather (LE) seats. **Handbrake:** stock, or a hydraulic drift handbrake with a strength slider.
- **Body styles (Body slot):** hatchback, coupe, convertible, or the race tube chassis.
  - Coupe / convertible: steel or carbon trunk lid, trunk spoilers (OEM rear wing with third brake light, ducktail lip).
  - Convertible: power soft top raised or folded; frameless doors with the windows up or lowered.
  - Headliner slot (hatch and coupe), so stripped builds can lose it.
- **License plates:** game-generated US plates (rear plate in a bumper recess; a front plate slot on the bumper).
- **Fuel:** stock 60 L tank, 40 L race cell, 10 L drag cell. **Exhaust:** stock or 3" race straight-through. **Radiator:** stock or race aluminium.

## In-game checklist (please report results)

I can't run BeamNG.drive in the build environment. The mod is checked by an offline validator: the part tree resolves, node references exist, flexbody vertex-to-node coverage, materials and textures exist, mass/CoG, an explicit-integration stability bound at 2000 Hz, and the powertrain graph. Some syntax could not be confirmed from the official docs, though. If something misbehaves, open the console (`~`) and send me the red lines.

1. **Spawn every configuration.** Check the console for JBeam or material errors.
2. **Turbo** (KA-T, SR20DET, Sleeper). The `turbocharger` section is UNVERIFIED. Check that boost builds (the turbo/boost UI app) and that the wastegate slider changes power (torque curve app).
3. **Automatic** (XE (A), SE (A)). Check the `automaticGearbox` and torque converter: P/R/N/D, kickdown, creep.
4. **Sequential** gearboxes (race builds). Check `sequentialGearbox` shifting.
   - The **transbrake** and **two-step** on the K20 Drag Spec: hold the transbrake, bring the revs up on the two-step, release.
   - The **line lock** should hold the fronts during a burnout.
   - The **wheelie bar** wheels should touch down on hard launches.
5. **Aero.** On the K20 Track Spec, the wing angle slider should visibly rotate the wing and change downforce (in-game aero debug).
6. **Thermals.** Engines use the template's air-cooled model; check that the temperature gauge moves and nothing overheats at idle.
7. **Lights.**
   - Low/high beam should raise the **pop-up headlights** (hydro on `lowhighbeam`).
   - Check the tail/brake/reverse/signal glow and the gauge backlight at night (hatch and coupe tail lamps).
   - **S14:** the fixed headlamps should light (zenki and kouki), the kouki tail lamps should glow in their smoked lenses,
     and the inner tail / reverse lamps should open with the trunk lid.
8. **Gauges.** The tach (left), speedo (right), temperature and fuel needles should follow the engine (S13 and S14 faces).
   From the driver's camera the cluster should sit under its hood and below the windshield, with the needles on the dials.
   The race tach should sit on the steering column.
9. **Crash damage.** Panels should come off at the hinges and latches, glass should crack (`s13_glass_dmg`), and lights should break.
   - Coupe / convertible: the trunk lid should hinge at the front and break its latch at the bottom.
   - Convertible: the raised soft top should stay latched to the windshield header at speed and tear off in a rollover.
     The body should feel a little less stiff than the coupe (no roof), but should not flex visibly on bumps.
10. **Materials.** Paint colour selection should work (palette `nullcolormaskR.color.png`), and the shared `s1x_*` materials should load from `vehicles/common/s1x_240sx/`.
    No part should look see-through or inside-out. Every closed part is checked for outward normals, and every config is
    ray-cast from the seats and around the car for surfaces seen from behind.
11. **Tuning menu.** Every slider should change something; ride height and camber should look right.

## Building from source

```bash
pip install -r tools/requirements.txt         # bpy 4.5 (headless Blender), numpy, scipy, pillow, pytest
python tools/fetch_reference.py               # BeamNG docs + official modding template (reference only)
python -m tools.build_all                     # textures -> jbeam -> meshes -> configs -> validate -> thumbs -> zip
python -m tools.validate.run -v               # validation report only
python -m tools.validate.normals              # inside-out (inverted) closed parts in the mesh library
python -m tools.validate.clearance --vehicle s14   # dash / cluster / race tach clear of the windshield
python -m tools.validate.backfaces --veh s14_240sx --all --hide-hood   # surfaces the game would cull, per config
python -m tools.render.preview_car out/ --views q_front_left,interior_cut,engine --cull   # --cull: back faces hidden, as in game
pytest tools/tests
```

Layout:

- `tools/jbeam/`: relaxed-JSON parser, writer, part-tree resolver.
- `tools/vehicle/`: JBeam generators. `common/` holds the shared engines, gearboxes, wheels, suspension and configs; `s13/` holds the S13 data and parts.
  `s14/` holds the S14 shape, dimensions and the S13 -> S14 warp (`warp.py`) that retargets the S13 parts, plus the S14-only parts and configs.
- `tools/model/`: Blender mesh generators (body loft, panels, mechanicals, interior) and the material registry.
- `tools/textures/`: procedural textures (gauges, tires, glass damage, fabrics, detail normals).
- `tools/validate/`: offline checks.
- `tools/render/`: preview renders and thumbnails.
- `mod/`: generated mod tree. Binary assets aren't committed; they're inside `dist/*.zip`.
